## 1 Purpose and environment
R1.1 Battery-powered Christmas tree ornament: a tree-shaped PCB sandwich, 70 x 105 mm, about 15 mm thick (14.0 mm boards plus gap, 14.8 mm with LEDs; D18), about 40 g with AAA cells, hangs from a hole at the tip.  source: notes, mock   check: (review)
R1.2 Indoor use, 0-40 C, a few weeks per year, lifetime of several seasons. Final housing: 3D-printed transparent shell around the sandwich.  source: eng, A   check: (review)
R1.3 Hanging hole is 1.4 mm through BOTH boards at the tip.  source: notes   check: DRC / board outline drawing
R1.4 BLE (nRF54L10 as assembled, D2; the nRF54L15 stays the alternate): time sync and effect selection from a phone; later firmware update over BLE (MCUboot DFU). Memory (2026-11-23): nRF54L10 has 1 MB NVM (datasheet table 1012 kB RRAM, snippet) and 192 kB RAM against 1.5 MB / 256 kB on the L15 (Nordic product pages, level page). The image of MCUboot + app + second slot or swap area and the VPR coprocessor image must fit: not yet sized (bootloader and VPR sizes not read).  source: notes, eng, D2   check: (review, firmware hand-off; PARTS-REVIEW tree-a finding 23; bring-up: read the memory sizes over SWD)

## 3 Power cycle and autonomy
| Load | State | Hours/day | Current | Fits deep sleep |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Effects window | LEDs lit, BLE advertising, buck-boost at 3.5 V | 11 (16:00-24:00, 06:00-09:00) | LED average capped by firmware at about 2 mA, plus CPU/clock | no (running) |
| Sleep window | RTC + 32.768 kHz only, buck-boost at 3.0 V | 13 (00:00-06:00, 09:00-16:00) | target below 5 uA for the whole board | yes |
R3.1 Schedule: effects 16:00-24:00 and 06:00-09:00, sleep otherwise; time from the phone (no battery-backed RTC beyond the LFXO; time is lost when the cell is removed).  source: notes   check: (review, FIRMWARE-RULES.md)
R3.2 Autonomy on 2 x AAA alkaline (series: 1250 mAh, 80 % usable, firmware cut-off 2.0 V at the pack = 1.0 V per cell (D22), TPS63900 UVLO 1.65-1.75 V) at 11 h/day: at least 20 days with the LED average at 2 mA (gate FAIL below 20 days); firmware default LED average 1.0 mA gives about 36 days (gate FAIL below 30 days on that row). Numbers: calc/tree_power.py. The notes' 'about 40 days' ignored the boost from the cell to the 3.5 V rail (about 1.58 x), the series connection and the cut-off: restated on 2026-11-23, decision D19.  source: notes (restated), tree_power.py, D19, D22   check: calc/tree_power.py rows 'autonomy break-even' (FAIL below 20 days) and 'autonomy at firmware default LED' (FAIL below 30 days); calc/power_budget.py autonomy row
R3.3 Sleep current of the whole board < 5 uA at 3.0 V (RTC, TPS63900 75 nA Iq, VBAT divider 2 x 2.2 M with 100 nF (D21; about 0.7 uA at 3.0 V), leakage of the LED matrix zero because all lines are Hi-Z).  source: A (5 uA)   check: calc/power_budget.py sleep sum; calc/tree_power.py 'sleep current at the cell' (profile still holds the 2 x 1 M divider until the D21 netlist change lands)
R3.4 Cells. Variant A: 2 x AAA alkaline in series 1.8-3.2 V, reverse-polarity protection by the high-side P-MOSFET Q1 (D7, both variants). Variant B: one protected Li-ion pouch about 500 mAh on JST PH, charged by MCP73831 at about 100 mA from USB-C (power only, 5.1 k CC pull-downs), charge only between 0 and 45 C is a firmware/bring-up point (no thermistor on board: noted as residual risk).  source: notes, parts, D7   check: calc/tree_power.py (cell range, 'P-FET drop', 'P-FET gate drive', 'VIN at cut-off', 'MCP73831 dissipation'); charge window (review, FIRMWARE-RULES.md); calc/buck.py does not apply
R3.5 Rail: TPS63900 buck-boost, VDD 3.0 V when idle (SEL low), 3.5 V while LEDs are lit (SEL high). The CFG resistors only select the preset (1 % / 200 ppm parts, total error under 3 %); output accuracy is +-1.5 % (TPS63900 6.5), so 3.4475-3.5525 V. nRF54L recommended VDD max is 3.6 V (3.4 V above 85 C), absolute maximum 3.9 V (limits from the nRF HTML summary: UNVERIFIED against the PDF; the QFN pin table was confirmed against Nordic's reference layout v0.8).  source: notes, TPS63900 datasheet, nRF PS (HTML summary, unverified)   check: calc/tree_power.py 'VDD lit worst case' (margin to 3.6 V) and 'SEL step timing'; calc/buck.py does not apply (CFG resistors, not a feedback divider)

## 4 LED matrix
R4.1 16 RGB LEDs, common anode (XL-1010RGB-YG, 1 x 1 mm), 48 dies on 8 GPIO lines (charlieplex). Each line is the anode of 2 RGB LEDs; their 6 cathodes sit on 6 distinct lines of the other 7. Exactly one resistor per line. D1-D8 on board B (outer face), D9-D16 on board A (outer face). Line n is the anode of D(n) on B and D(n+8) on A; this is an assignment, not a position mirror (D14: no x mirror in the stack); A's cathode offsets are the negatives of B's (ARCHITECTURE.md).  source: notes, mock, A (anode pairing)   check: calc/led_matrix.py (every die addressable once, 6 distinct cathode lines)
R4.2 One die lit at a time, slots about 100 us, 200 Hz frame, 8-bit PWM per slot, gamma and white balance in firmware; constant current per die, built-in power cap.  source: notes   check: (review, FIRMWARE-RULES.md); calc/led_matrix.py gives the per-die current
R4.3 No ghosting: a series chain of dies across a line pair that firmware can drive must need more than VDD(max) + 0.1 V (sum of the minimum Vf). Firmware rule: only the die pairs in tree_common.slot_table() are driven (never line a with line a+4). Sneak current at the worst Vf mix is a WARN with a bench item (ghost glow in the dark with a green die lit).  source: notes, led_matrix analysis   check: calc/led_matrix.py ghost rows
R4.4 Per-die current at 3.5 V with the real GPIO drive (P1 high drive, typical pin resistance assumption) must reach at least 1 mA for every colour at worst-case Vf (FAIL below). At the datasheet MINIMUM drive (100 ohm source + 133 ohm sink) green and blue reach only about 0.77 mA at worst Vf: known deviation, WARN, bench confirm (decision D13). Resistor 22 ohm; GPIO total under 15 mA.  source: notes, XL-1010RGB-YG, nRF PS (unverified)   check: calc/led_matrix.py (typical row FAIL, minimum-drive row WARN with known text)
R4.5 Which GPIO lines: 8 lines on port P1 (GPIOTE + timer scanning), assigned in the pin plan of ARCHITECTURE.md (single source gen/tree_common.py; the nRF54L10 assembled per D2 has the same QFN48 pin table, UNVERIFIED for the L10 beyond the HTML summary). Option P2 extra drive with VPR: needs the RAM and NVM check of R1.4.  source: notes, D2   check: calc/led_matrix.py (pin table rows), ARCHITECTURE.md pin table

## 5 Boards, interfaces and mechanics
R5.1 Two boards stacked, INNER faces towards each other, joined by one 2 x 4 2.54 mm header (8 LED lines, no power, no ground; J3 female SMD 8.5 mm on A, J4 male SMD on B, D3) and two M2 nylon screws with 12.0 mm standoffs (gap 12.0 mm, D18). A third support point is the header itself.  source: notes, eng, D3, D18   check: gen/tree_split_check.py (J3/J4 pads mate by position, hook 30-tree-split), gen/tree_outline.py --selftest (holes, header zone, gap; hook 40-tree-outline), calc/tree_power.py 'header mated gap'
R5.2 Board A (main, 4 layers): inner face = nRF54L10 (BOM, D2; schematic symbol nRF54L15, same pin table), crystals, chip antenna, TPS63900, battery holder (variant A) or charger and USB-C (variant B), J3; outer face = 8 RGB LEDs, SW1, Tag-Connect TC2030-NL. Board B (LEDs): 8 RGB LEDs on its OUTER face only; header male part on the inner face.  source: notes, mock, D2, D4   check: gen/tree_split_check.py (LEDs and header per board), BOM of each board, BOARD.md
R5.3 Antenna at the tree tip (Johanson 2450AT42A100E, D12) with a no-copper keep-out on both boards (top 17 mm, `ANT_KEEPOUT_H_MM` in gen/tree_outline.py; the part's own no-ground area is larger than our tip: bench tune), pi-match pads for tuning. Because LEDs have copper, the star LEDs D1 and D9 sit just below the keep-out, not at the very tip (decision D8: the keep-out wins).  source: notes, mock, D8, D12   check: gen/tree_outline.py --selftest (keep-out and star LED rows, hook 40-tree-outline); DRC rule area and calc/critical.py at gate 4
R5.4 Mounting holes: two M2 at the ends of tier 3 ((10.7, 71.0) and (59.3, 71.0) mm, D17); gap 12.0 mm (D3, D18); 2 x AAA holder (62.2 x 22.4 x 11.3 mm, standing upright and centred, D15) or pouch overhanging into the gap (D16); USB-C opens at the bottom edge of board A; SW1 and Tag-Connect on A's outer face are reachable.  source: notes, mock, D15-D18   check: gen/tree_outline.py --selftest (holes, holder window, stack thickness; hook 40-tree-outline); 3D review at gate 4
R5.5 For the printed transparent housing the repo carries the STEP of the assembled stack, the outline DXF and a MECHANICAL.md (hole positions, component heights, keep-outs, USB-C and button openings). Today: MECHANICAL.md and tree_outline.dxf exist; the STEP is not produced yet.  source: eng   check: gate 6 (fab_package; STEP export of the finished stack is a gate 6 item, MISSING today)
R5.6 Wrong-mating protection: the header is keyed by the asymmetric outline and hole pattern and a mating test; a reversed header cannot damage anything (signals only, series resistor on every line).  source: hw-review checklist   check: calc/led_matrix.py mating test (mate_selftest), gen/tree_split_check.py (hook 30-tree-split)
