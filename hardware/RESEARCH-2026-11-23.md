## TPS63900DSKR (TI SLVSET3D Rev D)
- Pins: 1 EN, 2 SEL, 3 CFG1, 4 CFG2, 5 CFG3, 6 VOUT, 7 LX2, 8 GND, 9 LX1, 10 VIN, 11 thermal pad (GND). EN, SEL, CFG1-3 must never float (Table 5-1 p.3). No PG pin.
- SEL high = VO(2) (set by CFG1 + CFG2, Table 7-2); SEL low = VO(1) (set by CFG3, Table 7-3). SEL VIH 1.2 V / VIL 0.4 V (columns swapped in the printed table, UNVERIFIED reading).
- Presets: VO(1) 3.0 V = RCFG3 13.3 k. VO(2) 3.5 V = RCFG1 511 ohm and RCFG2 8.25 k (unlimited) or 10.5 k (100 mA input limit); 1 % or better, 200 ppm or better, total error under 3 % (pp.14-16). DC accuracy +-1.5 % (6.5 p.5): 3.5 V -> max 3.5525 V < nRF54L15 3.6 V.
- CFG resistors are read once at start-up; runtime changes ignored. SEL is read live: 30-40 us delay, then 100 mV steps of 125 us (about 0.66 ms up). No active discharge: 3.5 -> 3.0 V takes tsettle = Co x dV / Io (22 uF, 0.5 V, 1 uA = about 11 s). Firmware: set SEL low in good time before sleep.
- SEL start-up priority not stated: pull SEL down (3.0 V at start); EN tie to VIN or GPIO with pull.
- Inductor 2.2 uH only; Isat >= 1 A for settings <= 100 mA, >= 2 A unlimited; Ci >= 5 uF, Co >= 10 uF EFFECTIVE after DC bias (22 uF 0603 6.3 V X5R derates strongly at 3.5 V: check 0805 25 V part, Basic C45783). Layout: caps at VIN/VOUT, thermal pad soldered (10.1 p.31). Land: pitch 0.5, pad 0.25 x 0.6, thermal pad 1.2 x 2.0 (p.38).
- UVLO rising 1.73-1.77 V, falling about 1.65 V (hysteresis 100 mV): 2 x AAA stops delivering below that under load; end-of-life on/off cycling risk. Iq 75 nA typ, 1 uA max (unlimited setting). Efficiency about 87-88 % at 1.8 V in, 3.3 V out, 1-10 mA (Fig 8-7).
- Not stated: current limit tolerance, VO=3.5 V efficiency/ripple, DVS overshoot, shutdown VIN-VOUT leakage path.

## MCP73831T-2ACI/OT (Microchip DS20001984H)
- Pins: 1 STAT, 2 VSS, 3 VBAT, 4 VDD, 5 PROG. IREG = 1000 / RPROG(k): 100 mA = 10 k. Vreg 4.2 V +-0.75 %. Cin >= 4.7 uF, Cout >= 4.7 uF on VBAT (also with no cell). VDD 3.75-6 V. STAT is tri-state push-pull (NOT open drain): High-Z in shutdown/no battery, LOW while charging, HIGH when complete. Precondition 10 % of IREG, termination 7.5 %. No statement about protected pouches; absent-battery detect needs >7 Mohm at VBAT.
- Thermal: SOT-23-5 130-230 C/W; dissipation (VDD - Vbat) x I: compute with a script, not by hand.

## nRF54L15 (Nordic datasheet v1.0, Sept 2025; combined with nRF54L10/L05). NO PDF obtained (Cloudflare 403); values are from the HTML datasheet via a summarising fetch tool: a human must re-check the numbers that drive a decision against the PDF.
- Same QFN48 pin table covers L15/L10/L05 (pin compatibility therefore documented in the one datasheet). L10 flash/RAM smaller than L15: check against the firmware size (MCUboot + BLE).
- Pins: 1 P1.00/XL1, 2 P1.01/XL2 (LFXO), 3 P1.02/NFC1, 4 P1.03/NFC2, 5-9 P1.04-P1.08, 10 VDD, 11-21 P2.00-P2.10 (P2.06-P2.10 = TRACE, P2.07 also SWO), 22 VDD, 23 P0.00, 24 P0.01, 25 SWDIO, 26 SWDCLK, 27 P0.02, 28 P0.03, 29 P0.04, 30 nRESET, 31 ANT, 32 VSS_PA, 33 DECRF (must connect to DECA), 34 XC1, 35 XC2, 36 VDD, 37-42 P1.09-P1.14, 43 DECA, 44 VSS, 45 DECD, 46 DCC, 47 VDD, 48 VDD, 49 exposed pad VSS.
- Ports: P0 (5 pins) and P1 wake from System OFF/ON (SENSE/DETECT), have GPIOTE (P1: 8 channels, P0: 4). P2 has extra drive (E0E1) but NO GPIOTE and NO wake. Free GPIO with LFXO fitted: P0.00-04, P1.04-P1.14, P2.00-10 = 27. ADC inputs: AIN0-3 on P1.04-P1.07, AIN4-7 on P1.11-P1.14 (P1.08 = EXTREF).
- Drive (VDD >= 1.7 V): standard IOH/IOL 1 / 3 / 4 mA at VDD-0.4 V / VSS+0.4 V; high drive source min 4 mA, sink min 3 mA at 0.4 V; extra drive (P2) 14 mA source / 16 mA sink. No output resistance, no VOL for high drive. Recommended maximum for ALL GPIOs together: 15 mA. VIH 0.7 VDD, VIL 0.3 VDD.
- VDD operating 1.7-3.6 V (extended temp 3.4 V); ABSOLUTE MAX 3.9 V; pin voltage <= VDD + 0.3 V. (Requirement R3.5 said 3.6 V is the max: that is the recommended operating limit.) POR min 1.75 V, BOR 1.56-1.64 V.
- Current (3 V, DC/DC, 25 C): System ON idle with GRTC + LFXO 1.5 uA (64 KB RAM) / 1.9 (128 KB) / 2.9 (256 KB); System OFF with GRTC+LFXO 0.9 uA; CPU run 2.6 mA; radio RX 3.4 mA, TX 0 dBm 4.8 mA; Constant Latency mode 0.55 mA (keep OFF). Advertising average: not stated. The chip has a GRTC, not an RTC.
- LFXO: CL 6-9 pF, ESR max 100 k, internal load caps 3-18 pF (INTCAP, set in firmware before start; no external load caps in Nordic's reference). HFXO: CL 6-9 pF.
- SWD: SWDIO pin 25 (14 k pull-up), SWDCLK pin 26 (14 k pull-down), nRESET pin 30 (pull-up inside). Tag-Connect needs VDD, SWDIO, SWDCLK, nRESET, GND (+ optional SWO P2.07).
- Config 1 (QFAA, DC/DC from battery, no NFC): C1 2.2 uF, C2 2.2 uF, C4/C7/C8 100 nF, C10 100 nF optional, C12 10 nF (0201), L1 4.7 uH (0603), FB1 ferrite 0201, X1 32.768 kHz 2012, X2 32 MHz 2016, L2 LQP03HQ2N7B02 (2.7 nH), L3/L4 LQP03HQ3N5B02 (3.5 nH), C6 GJM0335C1E1R5WB01 (1.5 pF), C9 GJM0335C1E2R0WB01 (2.0 pF). WIRING (which cap on which pin) NOT read: needs the schematic image from the PDF / reference design files. "Pay attention to how C6 is grounded": through VSS_PA pin 32 and the die pad, not to the plane directly.
