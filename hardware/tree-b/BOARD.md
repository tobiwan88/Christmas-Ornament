## Purpose
Board B is the LED board of the ornament: 8 RGB LEDs D1-D8 on its outer face (F.Cu) and the male 2 x 4 header J4 on the inner face (B.Cu). No IC, no power circuit. Contract with board A (../ARCHITECTURE.md): same outline, hole pattern and header position as tree-a; J4 mates with J3 by position (D14, no mirror), carrying the 8 LED lines only, no power and no ground; line n is the anode of D(n) on B (and of D(n+8) on A). Both boards are 4 layers, 1.0 mm (one customer panel, one stackup; B's inner layers are GND and carry no signal). Setup, stencil and fees are paid on tree-a's order row (`fab.fees_paid_by: ../tree-a`). Requirements: ../REQUIREMENTS.md; order notes: ../ORDER.md.

## Decisions (dated; why)
Full list: ../DECISIONS.md. Those that shape this board (all 2026-11-23, engineer delegated end to end):
- D1, D5, D17: one customer panel with A; 4 layers 1.0 mm JLC04101H-7628 although B only needs outer layers; B nested and turned 180 degrees in the panel (CPL rotated by assembly).
- D3: J4 male SMD vertical header C192300 (alternate C32713284 has a different land); mated height 8.5 + 3.2 = 11.7 mm in the 12.0 mm gap (D18), plastic height UNVERIFIED.
- D9, D14: line n is the anode of D(n) on B; J4 pin n carries the net of the J3 pad it touches (J4 on B.Cu is mirrored by KiCad, so J4 pin 1 touches J3 pin 2).
- D13: same LED and ghost margin as board A (firmware slot rule, ../FIRMWARE-RULES.md rule 1).
- DS-A1 (review 2026-11-23): LED footprint follows the datasheet top view (1 R top-left, 2 G top-right, 3 anode bottom-left, 4 B bottom-right).
- D20, D23: cost cap and model are those of tree-a's order.

## Pitfalls specific to this board
- J4 sits on B.Cu: KiCad mirrors it, so J4 pin 1 touches J3 pin 2 (LED_L8). The schematic pin map follows `tree_common.j4_pin_net()`; do not "fix" it to match J3 pin numbers.
- J4 plastic height 3.2 mm and its pad width are readings of the Boom drawing (UNVERIFIED, PARTS-REVIEW B1/B2).
- Fab footprint fit (`fab_package.py --fit-only`) runs only after the board file exists (gate 4).
