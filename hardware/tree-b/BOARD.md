Status (current state first; history below, dated): gates 3-5 built; 2026-11-24 D31 (proposed, engineer to confirm): board B has a GND net, two plated M2 mounts MH3/MH4 with GND rings tie it to A's GND (brass standoffs), In1/In2 are solid GND planes; routed with KiCadRoutingTools v0.24.2 in 11 s (157 tracks, 26 signal vias, 640 mm, 66 GND vias); DRC 0 / 0 / 0 at all severities with parity; schematic ERC 0 / 0, split proof OK (49 rows); `HW_BOARDS=hardware/tree-b scripts/hw-check.sh` and `--strict` print `hardware checks: OK` (the full gate currently stops at tree-a critical, board A work in progress). No power circuit on this board. Open decisions: ../DECISIONS.md and PARTS-REVIEW.md.

## Purpose
Board B is the LED board of the ornament: 8 RGB LEDs D1-D8 on its outer face (F.Cu) and the male 2 x 4 header J4 on the inner face (B.Cu). No IC, no power circuit. Contract with board A (../ARCHITECTURE.md): same outline, hole pattern and header position as tree-a; J4 mates with J3 by position (D14, no mirror), carrying the 8 LED lines only, no power and no ground; line n is the anode of D(n) on B (and of D(n+8) on A). Both boards are 4 layers, 1.0 mm (one customer panel, one stackup; B's inner layers are solid GND planes and carry no signal, D31). Setup, stencil and fees are paid on tree-a's order row (`fab.fees_paid_by: ../tree-a`). Requirements: ../REQUIREMENTS.md; order notes: ../ORDER.md.

- D31 (2026-11-24, user: "board B misses totally ground"; proposed, engineer confirms): B gets GND. Two plated M2 holes MH3/MH4 (Ø2.2 drill, Ø4.4 ring on both faces, net GND, inside the Ø5 keep-out, at (10.7, 71.0) / (59.3, 71.0)); brass 12.0 mm standoffs with nylon screws tie B's GND to A's GND (MH1/MH2 on A must be the same plated GND rings); In1 and In2 are solid GND planes (stackup roles restored), cut out at the antenna keep-out and 0.5 mm back from the outline and holes; GND vias along the edge (fence) and between the planes; the header stays 8 lines. ## Routing
Router: KiCadRoutingTools v0.24.2 (hardware/.tools, tools/setup_tools.sh), driven by `gen/tree_b_krt.py` (flow "chain" from the KRT README: `route_planes.py` pours the GND planes and an edge via fence, then `route.py --nets "*"` routes all nets; settings in `tree_b_krt.CHAIN`, written down there). Result frozen in `frozen-routes.json` (board coordinates, `_comment` says how) and replayed as LOCKED copper by `gen/tree_b_pcb.py`, so a rebuild is identical (KRT itself is deterministic for equal inputs). The own router (`gen/tree_b_route.py` + `tree_b_astar.c`, 40 seeded trials, about 3 minutes) is archived as a fallback; its last result is `frozen-routes-own-router.json` (replay with `tree_b_pcb.py --frozen hardware/tree-b/frozen-routes-own-router.json`; it was routed before the GND planes and has no GND vias, so it fails the GND fence row).
- Flow: the generator writes the unrouted board with a 1.3 mm F.Cu stub of 0.25 mm on every LED pad (`tree_b_pcb.py --no-routes --stub 1.3`; the pads are 0.45 mm with 0.35 mm gaps and 0.8 mm pitch, nothing fits between them, the stub leaves the pad and KRT starts at its far end), KRT routes, `tree_b_krt.py` extracts tracks and vias and grades them (KiCad DRC at all severities with parity, `via_pad_gap`), writes the frozen file only when DRC / unconnected / parity are 0 and every via drill edge is >= 0.2 mm from every SMD pad (own net included; KiCad's `hole_clearance` skips same-net pairs, so this is proved geometrically, in `--proof` too).
- KRT flags that matter: `--escalation off` (no thinner tracks or smaller vias than ours: no 0.1 mm tracks), `--layers F.Cu In1.Cu In2.Cu B.Cu --layer-costs 1 -1 -1 1` (signals stay on the outer layers, the inner layers are planes), `--same-net-pad-clearance 0.2` (no via in a pad, with the stubs), `--grid-step 0.05`, `--via-cost 200`, `--heuristic-weight 1.5`, `--power-nets GND --power-nets-widths 0.25`, route_planes `--stitch-edge-fence --stitch-fence-pitch 4.2`. Pitch 4.2 was chosen by measurement: 4.5 / 4.3 / 4.0 / 3.8 left a fence gap above the 5.0 mm limit near the shoulders or an open net (see ../NOTES-routing-lessons.md).
- Rules used: track 0.25 mm, clearance 0.15 mm (fab floor 0.127), via 0.6 / 0.3 mm (floor 0.45 / 0.2), copper to edge >= 0.3, via to edge 0.35 (custom rule), hole to hole 0.5, via drill to SMD pad >= 0.2 (custom rule `via clear of SMD pads, also same net`). Units in `.kicad_dru` values are required by KiCad 10 (`0.35mm`): without them the whole file was silently not enforced until 2026-11-24.
- Layers: signals on F.Cu and B.Cu only; In1 / In2 are GND planes. 66 GND vias (edge fence and stitching) join the planes.
- Hand-drawn: none.

## Pitfalls specific to this board
- `.kicad_dru` values need units (`0.35mm`): KiCad 10 silently ignores the whole rule file otherwise.
- A second PWR_FLAG on GND is an ERC error (power output against power output): one flag, on MH3.
- B's mounts are MH3/MH4 so designators stay unique when A uses MH1/MH2 (netlist merge, panel BOM).
- `tree-b.kicad_pro` is rewritten by every build and by KiCadRoutingTools runs; restore it with `git checkout` before a commit if the diff is only noise.
- J4 sits on B.Cu: KiCad mirrors it, so J4 pin 1 touches J3 pin 2 (LED_L8). The schematic pin map follows `tree_common.j4_pin_net()`; do not "fix" it to match J3 pin numbers.
- J4 plastic height 3.2 mm and its pad width are readings of the Boom drawing (UNVERIFIED, PARTS-REVIEW B1/B2).
- Fab footprint fit (`fab_package.py --fit-only`, now with a mirrored fit for bottom parts, see report 2026-11-23) needs the EasyEDA footprints in `hardware/out/fab/easyeda/` (fetched once without `--offline`; empty in a clean checkout, then the gate prints MISSING and passes). With them fetched (2026-11-23) it FAILS for tree-b: (1) the 8 LEDs, C965834: the fab footprint numbers its pads 1 top-left, 2 bottom-left, 3 bottom-right, 4 top-right at 0.63 mm pitch, ours follows the maker datasheet (1 R top-left, 2 G top-right, 3 A bottom-left, 4 B bottom-right, 0.8 mm pitch, DS-A1): worst pad 0.72 mm; assembly places by the package, but a human must confirm the LED orientation against the mark and add a `GEOMETRY_OK` entry or fix the land (gate 6, engineer); (2) J4, C192300: the fab footprint has its pads 5.6 mm apart across (y +-2.8) and rotated 90 degrees against our 7.0 mm centres (maker drawing 9.0 mm overall, 2.0 mm pads; PARTS-REVIEW B1/B2): worst pad 0.705 mm; settle the real land (maker drawing vs the fab part) before the order.
