## Purpose
Board A is the main board of the ornament and the owner of the order (`fab.order_includes: [../tree-b]`): nRF54L10 (BOM, D2; schematic symbol nRF54L15) with crystals, DC/DC and the chip antenna at the tree tip, TPS63900 buck-boost (3.0 V idle / 3.5 V lit by SEL), reverse-polarity P-FET, the cell (variant A: 2 x AAA holder, first order; variant B: Li-ion pouch on JST PH, USB-C and MCP73831, DNP in order 1), 8 RGB LEDs D9-D16 on the outer face, SW1 and the Tag-Connect pads. Contract with the other boards (../ARCHITECTURE.md): same outline, holes and header position as tree-b, J3 female SMD 8.5 mm carries 8 LED lines, no power, no ground; D(n+8) shares anode line n with D(n) on board B; both boards 4 layers 1.0 mm, one customer panel (../panel/BOARD.md). Requirements: ../REQUIREMENTS.md; firmware rules: ../FIRMWARE-RULES.md; order notes: ../ORDER.md.

## Decisions (dated; why)
Full list with reversal costs: ../DECISIONS.md. The ones that shape this board (all 2026-11-23, engineer delegated end to end):
- D2: MCU assembled is nRF54L10-QFAA-R7 C44800139 (JLC stock 941); the nRF54L15 C42458750 (stock 0) is the alternate. Why stock. Open: flash/RAM fit for MCUboot + VPR (PARTS-REVIEW finding 23).
- D3, D18: header SMD female 8.5 mm here, male on B; gap 12.0 mm; 12 mm nylon standoffs. Why the only all-inner-face option JLC can assemble.
- D4, D15: holder MYOUNG C964881 (JLC-assembled), upright and centred in the trunk, top edge y 37.6-41.8 mm; footprints of the holder and the variant B parts overlap on purpose (a DRC rule permits it for those references).
- D5, D6: stackup JLC04101H-7628, no impedance fee; Standard assembly, two-sided.
- D7: Q1 SI2301CDS reverse-polarity P-FET on both variants (drain to battery, source to VIN).
- D10: first order = variant A; `bom-variant-b.csv` lists the Li-ion variant; variant switch procedure in ../ORDER.md.
- D12: antenna Johanson 2450AT42A100E at the tip with pi/tee tuning pads; feed end UNVERIFIED (finding 1).
- D13: R = 22 ohm, lit VDD 3.5 V, TPS63900 EN tied to VIN, STAT / VBUS dividers; minimum-drive worst case for green/blue is a WARN with a bench item.
- D14: no x mirror between A and B (both F.Cu point forward).
- D19, D22: autonomy 20 days at 2 mA LED average, 36 days at the 1.0 mA default; firmware cut-off 2.0 V at the pack.
- D20, D23: cost cap 250 USD per order of 5 panels; the gate counts parts, loading, setup, stencil, handling, fixture, joints and the panel fee; PCB + shipping is a manual quote.
- D21: review fixes (U3 and C31 on VIN, VBAT divider 2 x 2.2 M + 100 nF, R43/R45 pull-downs, 1 k series on BTN_SW1, TVS footprint if a Basic/Preferred part exists, C9 ground return, variant B input back-power deferred).

## Pitfalls specific to this board
- kicad-sch-api 0.5.5 writes KiCad `(property private ...)` library fields unquoted (TC2030 symbol): the sheet will not load. `gen/tree_sch.py` `mark_dnp()` repairs it; keep that repair when the library changes.
- kicad-sch-api draws random uuid4s; `tree_sch.stable_uuids()` replaces them per file so a rebuild is byte-identical (symbol paths stay stable for the PCB). The title-block date is the run date.
- Power rails other than GND/VDD/VBUS are `power:VCC` symbols whose value is the net (BAT, VIN): KiCad names the net after the value.
- Designators differ from the Nordic reference: here L3/L4/L5 = reference L2/L3/L4 (L2 is the TPS63900 inductor), R9 = reference R1 (R1-R8 are the line resistors).
- Fab footprint fit (`fab_package.py --fit-only`) runs only after the board file exists (gate 4).
