# Firmware rules from the hardware (v0.1, 2026-11-23)

Rules the hardware depends on. Each names the requirement it serves (REQUIREMENTS.md), the source (datasheet section, calc row,
review item) and how it is checked. Register and pin names are checked against the datasheet when the driver is written.
Pins and nets: ARCHITECTURE.md (single source `hardware/gen/tree_common.py`). Decisions: DECISIONS.md. A rule marked BENCH has no
number from a datasheet yet: the first board confirms it (bring-up list in ORDER.md).

| # | Rule | Serves | Source / check |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Slot rule. Each LED slot drives exactly one die from `tree_common.slot_table()`: anode line high, cathode line low, every other line Hi-Z. Never drive a pair of lines a and a+4 (mod 8): there is no die there, and the red+red series chains (3.6 V at minimum Vf) that would ghost span exactly those pairs. | R4.3 (no ghosting), R4.2 | `tree_common.FIRMWARE_SLOT_RULE`; `calc/led_matrix.py` rows "R4.3 literal 3.60 V vs 3.652 V" and "sneak current worst" (WARN, bench: ghost glow in the dark with a green die lit); PARTS-REVIEW tree-a finding 10 |
| 2 | One die at a time. Slots about 100 us, frame 200 Hz, 8-bit PWM per slot, gamma and white balance in firmware (green is about 5 x brighter than red/blue per mA). Never two slots at once and never an unconfigured line between slots. | R4.2, R4.4 (GPIO total under 15 mA) | `calc/led_matrix.py` per-die current rows; XL-1010RGB-YG datasheet p4, p6 |
| 3 | SEL timing. SEL high only when the lit window starts; wait at least 1 ms after SEL high before lighting (rail steps 3.0 -> 3.5 V in 5 x 150 us + 40 us = 0.79 ms). Set SEL low before the sleep window starts: the output has no active discharge and decays only through the load, 8.8 s from 3.5 V to 3.0 V at 1.5 uA, so the sleep budget (5 uA) holds only after that time. Never leave SEL floating; the external 1 M pull-down gives 3.0 V at start. | R3.3, R3.5 | TPS63900 datasheet 7.3.5, 6.5; `calc/tree_power.py` row "SEL step timing"; PARTS-REVIEW tree-a finding 11 |
| 4 | Pack cut-off 2.0 V at the pack (1.0 V per cell). Compare VBAT_SENSE with 2.0 V (the reading sags under load by the path drop, 0.86 ohm model in the profile: take it in the same load state every time); when the pack is below 2.0 V stop lighting, set SEL low and go to the lowest sleep state. The re-arm threshold (hysteresis) is not decided: firmware parameter, must stay above the cut-off and the TPS63900 UVLO restart level (1.77 V rising at an unloaded cell). | R3.2 (autonomy, D22), R3.4 | `board.yaml` `design.cell.vcutoff_firmware_v`; `calc/tree_power.py` row "VIN at cut-off" (WARN, margin 114-166 mV to the 1.8 V recommended minimum); PARTS-REVIEW finding 12 |
| 5 | VBAT divider (2 x 2.2 M with 100 nF, D21, unswitched). Sample VBAT_SENSE (P1.04, AIN0) only after the 100 nF has settled (time constant = divider source resistance x 100 nF; the number is not in `calc/tree_power.py` yet: open item for its owner) and use the SAADC input with the long acquisition time that the source impedance needs. If a later revision switches the divider (P2.02 VBAT_DIV_EN, reserved), enable it, wait the settle time, sample, disable. | R3.3 (sleep), R3.2 | REVIEW-2026-11-23 DS-B2, DS-B3; PARTS-REVIEW finding 17 |
| 6 | LFXO start. Set the internal load capacitance (INTCAP) for the 9 pF crystal before starting the LFXO, start it, and use the GRTC and any time-keeping only after the "started" event. Do not enter the sleep window with the 32.768 kHz clock not running. The crystal's CL (9 pF) is at the nRF maximum: check start-up at 1.7 V and 40 C on the bench. | R3.1 (schedule from phone time), R3.3 | nRF PS LFXO (HTML summary, UNVERIFIED); PARTS-REVIEW finding 7; bring-up list |
| 7 | No VBUS_DET / CHG_STAT sensing until VDD is up (variant B). With VBUS present and VDD = 0 the ESD diodes of P1.10 and P0.00 back-power the chip (43 uA per pin, DS-B1, deferred hardening): keep those two pins disconnected (no pull, no SENSE) until VDD is stable, then enable. | DS-B1 (REVIEW), R3.4 variant B | REVIEW-2026-11-23 DS-B1; bring-up item "variant B pin levels" |
| 8 | Variant A: VBUS_DET (P1.10) and CHG_STAT (P0.00) have no charger behind them. The 120 k pull-downs (R43, R45, D21) hold them low; configure both as plain inputs with no pull and no SENSE wake (build-time variant flag). Never enable the internal pull-up on either pin in any variant: it would fight the divider. | R3.4, DS-B5 | REVIEW-2026-11-23 DS-B5; `gen/tree_pin_divider.py` |
| 9 | STAT sawtooth. MCP73831 STAT can toggle while charging a deeply discharged or absent cell. Do not use STAT edges as events; sample the level, debounce, and act only on a level that is stable. Treat Hi-Z / flicker as "no charge info", never as "full". | R3.4 variant B | REVIEW-2026-11-23 DS-C6 (behaviour UNVERIFIED, bench item C7); MCP73831 datasheet 1.0 STAT table |
| 10 | LED average current. Cap the LED average at 2.0 mA at the 3.5 V rail (hard limit); default 1.0 mA. The cap is the basis of the autonomy numbers: 2.0 mA gives 22.2 days, 1.0 mA about 36 days. | R3.2 (D19) | `calc/tree_power.py` rows "autonomy break-even" (FAIL below 20 days), "autonomy at firmware default LED" (FAIL below 30 days) |
| 11 | All LED lines Hi-Z in sleep (and before System OFF), CHG_STAT / VBUS_DET as in rule 7 or 8, TPS_SEL low. No GPIO drives a load in the sleep window. | R3.3 (< 5 uA), R4.3 | `calc/power_budget.py` and `calc/tree_power.py` sleep rows (3.72 uA typ, 4.67 uA with maxima) |
| 12 | Hand-off at boot: SEL low, all LED lines Hi-Z, clock started (rule 6), cut-off checked (rule 4), only then the lit window. | R3.1, R3.5 | rules 3, 4, 6 |

## Not decided here (firmware owner)
- Re-arm threshold after the cut-off (rule 4).
- SAADC acquisition time and the settle number for rule 5 (needs a row in `calc/tree_power.py`).
- Whether charging is blocked outside 0-45 C: no thermistor on the board (R3.4); residual risk, firmware can only use a bench-measured proxy.
